# Home page wireframe — `/`

## Desktop layout
- Shared public header with restored Auto Dealer wordmark, Services, Service Centers, About Us, account actions, and prominent booking action.
- Two-column hero: left side has a Delhi two-wheeler care label, “Your ride. Our responsibility.” headline, concise service promise, **Book a service** and **Explore services** actions, and rider proof; right side has the optimized workshop image, dark overlay, caption, and trust callout.
- Trust metrics strip for expertise, services completed, locations, and rider satisfaction.
- Three popular service cards with a link to the full service catalogue.
- About/benefits section, three-step service explanation, and three Delhi center cards with address, map, phone, and booking links.
- Customer testimonial and final service-reminder/register call to action; shared footer follows the page.

## Mobile layout
- Header navigation collapses to an accessible menu; account and booking links stay reachable.
- Hero text and actions precede the responsive image; actions stack and remain full-width touch targets.
- Metric strip, service cards, center cards, about content, and testimonial reflow to a single column without horizontal overflow.

## Interactions and states
- Booking routes through the customer demo sign-in gate when needed; service cards open `/services/:id`.
- Service-centre map links open the matching map search in a new tab; center booking links start booking.
- Header links open the catalogue, center section, or about section. The sign-in/register controls use the demo account interface.
