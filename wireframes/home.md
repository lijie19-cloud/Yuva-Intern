# Home page wireframe

## Desktop layout
- Sticky top navigation: wordmark, Services, service centers, Contact, Sign in, and primary Book service action.
- Hero: left aligned headline “Your Ride. Our Care. Every Journey.”, concise Delhi two-wheeler service promise, Book a Service and Explore Services actions; right side uses a cropped workshop/vehicle image with descriptive alt text.
- Trust strip: dealership experience, trained technicians, and transparent estimates.
- Popular services: six compact service cards with name, short description, starting price, and details link.
- Why choose us: three benefit cards.
- How it works: choose vehicle, select service and center, confirm appointment.
- Featured Delhi service centers with address and hours.
- Testimonials, contact CTA, and footer with contact details and useful links.

## Mobile layout
- Compact header with logo and an accessible menu button; navigation opens as a vertical panel.
- Hero image follows the headline and CTAs; CTAs stack full width.
- Service and benefit cards become a single-column list; steps and testimonials remain readable without horizontal scrolling.

## Interactions
- Book a Service opens the booking flow (login/demo account gate as applicable).
- Explore Services opens `/services`.
- Service cards open `/services/:id`; center/contact links open their relevant information.
